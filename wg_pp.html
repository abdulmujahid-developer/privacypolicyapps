Privacy Policy

Wingy Zoo · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Short Version: Wingy Zoo does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Abdul Mujahid ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Wingy Zoo (the "Game", "App"), published on Google Play and Huawei AppGallery.

By downloading, installing, or playing Wingy Zoo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Wingy Zoo is designed to be privacy-friendly. We do not collect, request, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data (GPS or approximate)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID)
  • Photos, contacts, calendar, or files on your device
  • Microphone or camera access
  • Account credentials or login information
  • Browsing history or app usage analytics

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Wingy Zoo saves your game progress only on your device using Android's built-in SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. It includes:

  • Your highest score
  • Coins earned in the game
  • Unlocked birds, worlds, and pipes
  • Currently selected items
  • Sound and vibration preferences
  • Last daily bonus claim time

You can delete all this data at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's data from your device settings.

4. Device Permissions

Wingy Zoo requests only one permission:

  • VIBRATE — Used only to provide haptic feedback when you tap the screen or when a game event occurs. You can disable this at any time from the in-game Settings menu.

The App does not request internet, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other sensitive permission.

5. Third-Party Services

Wingy Zoo does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools. There are no ads in the App.

If you download the App from Google Play or Huawei AppGallery, those platforms may collect certain information as part of their normal app distribution services. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Privacy Policy
  • Huawei Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

Wingy Zoo is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from anyone else. Because the App does not collect any personal data, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

7. Data Security

Since Wingy Zoo does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of your information being intercepted, breached, or misused by us. All game progress remains strictly on your own device.

8. International Users

Wingy Zoo does not transfer any data across borders. Your progress is stored locally on your device regardless of where you are in the world.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
