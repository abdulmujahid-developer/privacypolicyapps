Privacy Policy

ScreenLab — Screen Pixel Testor respects your privacy completely. This page explains how your information is handled.

Version 1.0.0 Effective: 2026 Developer: Abdul Mujahid
1

No Data Collection

ScreenLab does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. Nothing about you or your device leaves your phone.

2

Offline Operation

The app runs entirely offline and does not require internet access to function. All tests are performed locally on your device.

3

Local Storage Only

All preferences (theme, colors, test duration, haptic strength, custom colors, and other settings) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app removes all stored settings.

4

Permissions

The only permission requested is VIBRATE. It is used solely to provide haptic feedback during screen tests. No other permissions are requested.

5

Optional Email Feedback

If you tap "Email Us" inside the app, your email application opens with our address pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you personally press "Send" in your email app. Providing feedback is entirely optional.

6

Children's Privacy

ScreenLab is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7

Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this policy will be reflected inside the app and on this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
