Privacy Policy

VoraCam

Summary

VoraCam does not collect, upload, or share your personal data. All recordings stay on your device in a private app sandbox. Camera and microphone are used only while you are actively recording.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not track usage, analytics, or device identifiers. The app works entirely offline.

2. Permissions

Camera — required to record video. Microphone — required to record audio. Notifications — to show recording status while active. Overlay — optional, only if you enable the floating preview. All permissions are used exclusively for the stated purpose.

3. Storage & Recordings

All videos are stored in the app's private sandbox directory (/data/data/<package>/files/RecordedVideos/). They are not visible to other apps and are not backed up to any cloud. You can export them to a public folder at any time using the Export action.

4. Third Parties

No third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services are embedded in the app. No data leaves your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is no risk of exposure.

6. Your Rights

You can delete any recording at any time via the app's Recorded screen. Deleting a video removes it permanently from the sandbox.
