Privacy Policy

ScreenLab - Pixel Testor Pro
Developer: Abdul Mujahid Provider: Abdul Mujahid Last Updated: October 18, 2026
Your privacy is our priority. ScreenLab does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. This policy explains exactly what data the app does and does not handle.

1 No Data Collection

ScreenLab does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. There are no accounts, no logins, no cloud sync, and no telemetry of any kind.

2 No Internet Access

The app does not request or use internet permission. It runs fully offline. Nothing ever leaves your device. You can verify this yourself by reviewing the app's AndroidManifest permissions on the Play Store listing.

3 Local Storage Only

All preferences (theme, language, test colors, durations, haptic strength, favorites, custom colors, and other settings) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app removes all stored settings completely.

4 No Third-Party Services

ScreenLab contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporters, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The app is built entirely from Android platform libraries and Jetpack Compose.

5 Optional Email Feedback

If you tap Email Us in the side drawer, your email app opens with our address pre-filled: abdulmujahidpersonal@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you personally press "Send" in your own email app. The email contents are entirely up to you.

6 Permissions

ScreenLab requests the following permissions, and only these:

  • VIBRATE — used solely to provide haptic feedback during screen tests.
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY — used only if you enable the optional "Do Not Disturb during tests" toggle in Settings. This permission silences notifications while a test is running. It is never granted automatically — your system asks for your explicit approval before the feature works.
Note: ScreenLab does not request INTERNET, CAMERA, LOCATION, CONTACTS, MICROPHONE, STORAGE, or any other sensitive permission.

7 Children's Privacy

ScreenLab is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect any data at all, there is nothing to delete, export, or transfer.

8 Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this policy will be reflected on this page and inside the app. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision.

9 Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact:

Email: abdulmujahidpersonal@gmail.com
